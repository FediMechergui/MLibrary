Your library, your choices

Privacy information · Updated 11 November 2026 · App version 1.5

On your device

Books you import or download, reading progress, highlights, notes, quote designs and preferences are stored in this browser or app. MLibrary has no advertising SDK or analytics SDK. Clearing app or browser data removes local content. Your operating system may also back up app data under its own settings.

Optional device sync

Sync is off until you enable it. When enabled, shelves, reading progress, notes, highlights, quote designs and preferences are encrypted on your device with a recovery key before being stored on Netlify. Book files are not uploaded by this sync feature. The service receives an authentication token and a vault identifier, not the decryption key. Anyone who has your recovery key can access your synced library. We cannot recover a lost key.

Disconnecting a device stops its sync but keeps its local library. “Delete cloud copy” removes the active encrypted copy from the service. Disconnect your other devices first to prevent them uploading it again. Hosting backups and operational logs are managed under the hosting provider’s retention policies.

OCR and book sources

On-device OCR processes the selected image region locally. If you choose online OCR, that selected region and your chosen language are sent through our backend to OCR.space. MLibrary does not intentionally persist OCR images or extracted text on its server. The provider applies its own processing and retention policies. Book searches and download requests contact the selected libraries and their public services; they receive the query and network information needed to respond.

Exports and sharing

Backup files contain readable notes and, optionally, book files. They are not encrypted. Keep them private. Quote exports and backups are shared only through the destination you choose in your device’s share screen or download controls.

Hosting and support

Network services may process IP addresses, timestamps and technical request logs to provide the service. Do not place private information in catalogue searches. For support, use the developer contact provided by the distributor of your copy of MLibrary. A Play Store support contact will be added before store publication.

Provider information: Netlify privacy · OCR.space privacy. Book-specific rights and attribution remain available inside the reader.
